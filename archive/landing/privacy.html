Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

License & Device Information: When you activate PharmacyPro, we collect your email address (provided at purchase), license key, and a hardware fingerprint (HWID) used to bind your license to your device. We do not collect personal names, addresses, or payment card details.

Payment Processing: All payment data is handled exclusively by Paddle Billing (our Merchant of Record). We never see, store, or process your credit card information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Validate and manage your software license
  • Bind your license to your device for anti-piracy protection
  • Send license delivery emails containing your activation key
  • Respond to support inquiries
  • Generate anonymized analytics for product improvement

3. Data Storage & Security

License data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption. Hardware fingerprints are stored as one-way SHA-256 hashes and cannot be reversed to identify your specific hardware components. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share limited data with:

  • Paddle Billing: For payment processing and license fulfillment
  • Service Providers: Infrastructure and email delivery providers acting on our behalf under strict data protection obligations

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Request a HWID reset through the customer portal
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable

6. Data Retention

We retain license and activation data for the duration of your license and for a reasonable period afterward to support audits and customer service. Expired or revoked licenses may be purged from active systems after 12 months of inactivity.

7. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for session management. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or analytics cookies that track you across websites.

8. Children's Privacy

PharmacyPro is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to registered license holders.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at pharmacypro.support@gmail.com.
