Refund Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

1. Overview

PharmacyPro offers a one-time purchase license for desktop software. We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. This Refund Policy explains when and how you may request a refund.

2. Eligibility for Refund

You may request a full refund within 14 days of your original purchase date if:

  • The Software does not function as described on our product page
  • You experience a technical issue that our support team cannot resolve within a reasonable timeframe
  • You accidentally purchased the wrong product

3. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds will generally not be issued if:

  • The refund request is made more than 14 days after the purchase date
  • You have actively used the Software for an extended period and then request a refund
  • The request is based on a change of mind after successful use
  • The Software was purchased using a coupon or promotional discount code (test or promotional)

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact our support team at pharmacypro.support@gmail.com with the following information:

  • Your license key
  • The email address used at purchase
  • A brief description of the reason for your refund request

5. Refund Processing

Refunds are processed through Paddle Billing, our Merchant of Record. Once approved, refunds are typically credited to your original payment method within 5-10 business days. You will receive an email confirmation when the refund has been processed.

6. Effect of Refund

Upon a successful refund, your license key will be deactivated and you must cease all use of the Software. You are required to uninstall the Software from your computer and destroy any local copies.

7. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute without first contacting our support team, we reserve the right to permanently deactivate your license and restrict future purchases.

8. Contact

For refund requests or questions about this policy, email pharmacypro.support@gmail.com.
